## 8. Decorative / Landscaping Buildings

The wiki has limited data on decorative buildings. Individual pages for these do not exist or are stubs. Here is what is documented:

**Available Decorative Buildings:**
- Casino Resort
- Beacon
- Monument
- Fountain
- Library
- Theater
- Park
- College
- Hospital
- Shopping Center
- Statue
- Santa Sculpture

**Known Characteristics:**
- Purely cosmetic/morale buildings
- No direct gameplay impact on production or combat
- Placed on ground base
- Residential Areas provide the actual morale mechanic (higher levels = more morale)
- Landscaping buildings may contribute minor morale bonuses but exact values are undocumented on the wiki

**Recommendation for GDD**: Since GO2's decorative buildings had no significant mechanical impact, for Cryptomines Online consider either:
1. Skipping decorative buildings entirely in MVP
2. Making them provide small percentage bonuses (1-3% production boost) as a progression incentive
